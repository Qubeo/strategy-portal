Liechtenstein is not merely a convenient location for this work — it is a structurally privileged laboratory. Several characteristics converge to create unique conditions for prototyping regenerative digital ecosystems:

- **Governance agility** — As a micro-state, the distance between policy insight and policy implementation is extraordinarily short. The TVTG (Token and VT Service Provider Act), commonly known as the Blockchain Act, demonstrates this agility.
- **Legal innovation** — The Liechtenstein Venture Cooperative (LVC) legal form, under which DigiHub itself operates, provides a native vehicle for commons-based economic coordination.
- **Digital identity readiness** — E-ID infrastructure and progressive data protection laws align with the DID-based architecture of the ecosystem.
- **Bioregional embeddedness** — Situated in the Alpine region with deep connections to the DACH economies and EU/EFTA frameworks, Liechtenstein operates at the intersection of bioregional rootedness and international connectivity.
- **EDIH mandate** — As part of the European Digital Innovation Hub network, DigiHub Liechtenstein has an institutional mandate and network to translate local prototypes into European patterns.

The DigiHub's stated mission captures the ambition: *"We make Liechtenstein a lighthouse for positive digital transformation that connects humans through trust, transparency, and purpose-driven action."*
