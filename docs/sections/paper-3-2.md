<div class="flywheel-stage">
  <div class="flywheel-number">1</div>
  <div class="flywheel-content">
    <span class="flywheel-timeframe">Year −1 to 0</span>
    <h5>Awareness & Credibility</h5>
    <p>Organizations confront dependency vulnerability. Early movement members provide peer validation: "We faced the same crisis. Here's our journey." The sovereignty movement lends operational credibility — not financial, but experiential.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="flywheel-stage">
  <div class="flywheel-number">2</div>
  <div class="flywheel-content">
    <span class="flywheel-timeframe">Year 0–3</span>
    <h5>Coalition Formation</h5>
    <p>Organizations join communities of practice using common assessment frameworks. In isolation, each procurement budget is too small to influence vendors. Together, they represent a market that cannot be ignored. Procurement frameworks become shared standards.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="flywheel-stage">
  <div class="flywheel-number">3</div>
  <div class="flywheel-content">
    <span class="flywheel-timeframe">Year 3–5</span>
    <h5>Capability Building</h5>
    <p>Organizations develop expertise in different domains. Each contributes mastery to the commons while drawing on others' expertise. The cost of sovereignty drops dramatically when infrastructure is shared. This stage requires deep investment in organizational change — building sovereignty is cultural, not just technical.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="flywheel-stage">
  <div class="flywheel-number">4</div>
  <div class="flywheel-content">
    <span class="flywheel-timeframe">Year 5–8</span>
    <h5>Strategic Autonomy</h5>
    <p>Organizations achieve meaningful independence. They can credibly negotiate. Legacy vendors must adapt or lose market share. The power dynamic inverts from supplication to negotiation among peers.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="flywheel-stage">
  <div class="flywheel-number">5</div>
  <div class="flywheel-content">
    <span class="flywheel-timeframe">Year 8+</span>
    <h5>Normalization</h5>
    <p>Sovereignty disappears into normalcy. It is no longer a "project" — it is simply how things are done. Embedded in procurement policy, hiring practices, organizational culture. The ultimate success: when strategic autonomy becomes unremarkable.</p>
  </div>
</div>

DigiHub Liechtenstein currently operates at the boundary of Stages 1 and 2 — building awareness while simultaneously forming the coalitions and infrastructure needed for Stage 3.
