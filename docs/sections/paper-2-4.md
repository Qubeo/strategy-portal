The technical infrastructure of a regenerative digital ecosystem must itself embody commons principles: open protocols, interoperability, resistance to platform capture, and governance by participants.

The DigiHub operates on a stack built around several core components: decentralized identifiers (DIDs) providing self-sovereign identity; Matrix-based end-to-end encrypted collaboration spaces; AI "oracle services" that organizations can deploy, own, and monetize; and protocol-driven verification and credentialing systems.

Critically, this infrastructure is designed as a *civic protocol* — not a platform. The distinction matters: platforms extract value by becoming intermediaries; protocols enable value by becoming standards. The oracle marketplace model, where 80% of value flows to the service provider and the platform takes only a coordination fee, embodies this principle.
