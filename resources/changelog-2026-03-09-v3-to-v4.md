## Summary

V3 was an editorial/structural upgrade (persuasion architecture, belief shifts, success criteria). V4 is a **content-level** upgrade: it addresses what the paper *argues*, not how the brief is *organized*. The core finding: the ideas are strong but insufficiently positioned against the existing landscape, and several intellectual vulnerabilities are left unaddressed. V4 integrates honest acknowledgment of limits, competitor positioning, and the EU policy context the primary audience already inhabits.
