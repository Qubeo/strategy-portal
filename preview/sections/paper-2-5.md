The economic layer of the regenerative digital ecosystem draws on several convergent traditions that share a common insight: the dominant monetary monoculture is a root cause of both economic fragility and local economy impoverishment.

#### Collaborative Finance & Inside Money

The Collaborative Finance sphere, as articulated by Paolo Dini, builds on endogenous money theory and emphasizes strengthening "inside money" circuits through mutual credit systems, credit obligation schemes, and network clearing algorithms. This is not abstract theory — it is the operational logic of how bioregional economies can achieve monetary self-determination alongside digital self-determination.

#### The Commons Dividend

In the venture capital model, successful exits return capital to investors who deploy it in new startups. Value concentrates in fund managers. In the sovereignty model, successful transformations return *capability* to the commons, making future transformations easier. The first cohort pays the full cost of developing procurement templates, security frameworks, and migration playbooks. The second pays only adaptation costs. The tenth pays near zero — sovereignty becomes standard practice.

This is the Commons Dividend: shared infrastructure experiencing falling per-unit costs as membership grows, creating living knowledge that improves through use, maintained by a community that directly benefits from its quality.

#### Civic Protocol Economies

The Civic Protocol Economy framework explores innovations at the intersection of Civic Economies (relational, wellbeing-oriented) and Protocol Economies (enabled by smart contracts, DIDs, automated governance). A Civic Protocol Economy consists of relational economic systems, governed by their participants, represented through digital identities, that account for externalities, and are partially managed by digital agents.
